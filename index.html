What is MorphIQ?

MorphIQ is an ongoing scientific exploration into geometric and wave-based representation of computing primitives. Rather than claiming to replace standard von Neumann or imperative paradigms, our research explores parallel representations that map computational intent onto mathematical waveforms and hardware-bound primitives.

Our core focus revolves around intermediate representations (MorphIQ-IR), physical unclonable functions (PUF integration), and obfuscated recovery pathways. All disclosures represent non-validated research hypotheses currently under active prototyping.

MorphIQ-IR & Experiments

MorphIQ-IR serves as a spatial-spectral intermediate representation. Current experiments focus on signal attenuation, boundary integrity, and reconstruction fidelity under noisy computational environments.

Research Directions

  • Geometric features to spectral transformations
  • Waveform obfuscation and recovery semantics
  • Hardware PUF bindings for execution validation
