What is MorphIQ?

From geometry to computation.

MorphIQ is an experimental computational interface that investigates whether software instructions can be represented through geometric characteristics and signal-based encoding, instead of relying exclusively on conventional textual symbols.

The project is currently in an experimental prototype stage, focused on validating the architecture and the transformation pipeline.
